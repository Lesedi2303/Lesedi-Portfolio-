About me

I'm Lesedi Kgololo Sekamotho, and I'm a first-year student at Walter Sisulu University, doing a Diploma in ICT in Application Development (main stream). I'm still near the start of the journey, so I won't pretend to be an expert. What I do have is curiosity, and a habit of trying things until they work.

I want to build software that people actually use, whether that's an app that makes someone's day a little easier or a system that quietly does its job in the background. Right now I'm learning the foundations that everything else sits on.
